## 🛠️ Interactive Tools

### Cost of Living Calculator
**Location**: `/tools` → Cost Calculator tab

**How to Use**:
1. Enter your monthly income
2. Select lifestyle (Budget/Moderate/Comfortable/Luxury)
3. View detailed expense breakdown
4. See currency conversion (GEL ↔ GBP)

**Best For**: Budget planning, comparing costs with home country

### Neighborhood Comparison
**Location**: `/tools` → Neighborhood Comparison tab

**How to Use**:
1. Select up to 3 neighborhoods
2. Compare metrics: Safety, Transport, Restaurants, etc.
3. Filter by preferences
4. Read detailed descriptions

**Best For**: Choosing where to live, understanding areas

### Event Calendar
**Location**: `/community` or `/tools` → Events tab

**How to Use**:
1. Browse upcoming events
2. Filter by category (Social, Cultural, Business, etc.)
3. Click "Learn More & RSVP" to join
4. Track attendance numbers

**Best For**: Meeting expats, networking, social activities

### Community Finder
**Location**: `/community` or `/tools` → Community tab

**How to Use**:
1. Search for specific interests
2. Filter by category
3. View member counts and platforms
4. Click "Join Community" to connect

**Best For**: Finding your tribe, getting support, networking

### Weather Widget
**Location**: `/community` sidebar or `/tools` → Weather tab

**Features**:
- Current temperature and conditions
- Wind speed and humidity
- Visibility and pressure
- "Feels like" temperature

**Best For**: Daily planning, packing decisions
